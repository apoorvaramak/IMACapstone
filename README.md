# Rockability

## IMA Capstone Spring 2022 - Apoorva Ramakrishnan

# Description
This project was created to address the issue of the lack of accessibility in rhythm video games for people with physical disabilities. 
There are many rhythm games that already exist like Rock Band and Guitar Hero, which have controllers that would require two hands and 
arm movement. Other rhythm game controllers, like the standard Xbox controller or a tablet, require fine motor skills. These controllers 
allow for an immersive experience for people with these abilities, but not everyone has this luxury. I would like to address this discrepancy 
by creating a rhythm game that allows for a variety of controllers. These controllers will be created with differently-abled people in mind 
so that they can choose whichever controller they think would be best for their abilities. 

The entirety of my thesis can be viewed here: [Rockability Thesis](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1C0Q6PXm2qL1TW61U-Ex_ChSQLUg3_1xlYJ9c8vNKklU/edit?usp=sharing)

# Methodolody
While there are many different types of controllers for rhythm games, most of these are inaccessible to people with motor control function 
issues or people missing a hand or arm. The standard controller for many other video games is either a console controller or a mouse and keyboard,  
both of which are not accessible controllers for this demographic. Rhythm games have a wide variety of controllers that make the interactions and 
resulting gameplay completely different. Some examples are the full-size guitar shaped controllers for Guitar Hero and Rock Band, the dancing mats 
for Dance Dance Revolution, and the Oculus Touch controllers for Beat Saber. These controllers are all very different from each other and are part 
of the appeal of their respective games, proving that controllers are an important part of the rhythm game experience. Therefore, there needs to 
be a way to bring this experience of rhythm games with interactive and fun controllers to people that cannot use other controllers for accessibility 
reasons.
